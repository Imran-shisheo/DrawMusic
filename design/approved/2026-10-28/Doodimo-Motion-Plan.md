**Design review only. Explicit approval required before implementation.** The graphite/material reference is a presentation reel; the Mobbin clip is a success animation. Neither proves Doodimo navigation, cold-start behavior or frame rate. Its choreography is adapted into original Doodimo geometry rather than copied assets.

The matching final review board and storyboard are original built-in ImageGen **static mockups**, guided by the reviewed UI UX Pro Max skill and the actual reference analysis. They are not Stitch exports, app/prototype UI or recorded motion. The completed Stitch trials are preserved but are not the recommended review set.

## Opening: original mark → drawing → ready instrument

Proposed short sequence illustrated in the matching static storyboard:

|---|---|---|
|0–140ms|An original gesture/sound seed arrives on the persistent graphite underlay.|No artificial loading or empty black frame; real readiness remains authoritative.|
|140–440ms|The seed expands with elastic deformation into a dimensional drawing-to-sound form.|Original shapes/materials; no Phantom ghost, source username, speaker or album imagery.|

|900–1100ms|The instrument composition settles; bounded residual motion stops or becomes a subtle local scene.|This is an upper design proposal, not a required delay before drawing/navigation. Actual ready controls remain usable.|

Warm return does not replay a blocking splash. An input action can end decorative travel immediately. System and stored reduced-motion preferences produce the same ready composition with a static mark or brief opacity change, without expansion, orbit, confetti or a blank handoff.

## One continuous instrument surface

- **Home → editor → Back:** an actual selected artwork snapshot can travel through a single route Hero/proxy into the already anchored4:5 canvas. The actual drawing coordinate plane and audio cursor do not transform. Input cancels the presentation flight rather than waiting for it. Direct entry/missing source uses an opaque connected transition.
- **Library Drafts ↔ Exports:** keep the same header, backdrop, selector and both subsection states/scroll positions. Indicator and outgoing/incoming ink retarget from the current visible mixture. These are retained surface transitions, not pretend Navigator Heroes. RapidA→B→A and third-destination changes must maintain continuous coverage with no blank frame.
- **Voice/music/tools/background:** a bounded plate grows from its labeled trigger with related icon/selection movement. Surface shape and clipping connect the interaction; the canvas remains stable. Back reverses toward the trigger and restores focus. Native pickers/camera/share stay OS-owned.
- **Export:** the immutable preview/footer remain continuous through real settings, progress, cancel/error and ready states. A small original success flourish occurs only after actual atomic finalization. The source clip's celebration cannot become fake save/export progress.
- **Creative feedback:** press response, selection motion, bounded dimensional art and original glyph reactions belong to the same material language. Decorative motion is not an audio meter. Playback cursor/beat ownership remains native audio-clock driven.

## Timing, cost and evidence

Proposed component ranges: press100–140ms, selection180–220ms, connected panels240–320ms, route artwork300–420ms, reverse travel shorter when appropriate. Every animation must retarget/reverse safely from its actual painted state. Stable Hero namespace/document/revision ownership and inactive pointer/focus/semantics/ticker exclusion remain explicit.

Use bounded static authored lighting/materials and isolated transform/opacity/vector layers. Translucency is a material cue, not a demand for repeated full-screen blur. Cache lifetimes and scene visibility/lifecycle must remain bounded. Preserve Home's existing template document/delegate identity and paint isolation, retained Library contracts and canvas/input/provider separation.

After approval and native implementation, capture original real-time opening, tab switches, rapid reversals, Back, panels and reduced-motion paths on the exact source. Do not pad, retime or repeat video frames. Measure actual profile UI/raster timings independently from recording overhead and encoded export fps. A storyboard or rendered concept is not smoothness acceptance; no Android/iOS/physical performance claim is made here.
